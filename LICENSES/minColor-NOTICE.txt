It builds on others' work:

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Derived work: OpenDRT
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

The rendering (opendrt_kernel.h, opendrt_params.h, opendrt_presets.*,
opendrt_grade.h and the shims) is derived from OpenDRT v1.1.0
  Written by Jed Smith
  https://github.com/jedypod/open-display-transform
  License: GPLv3

minColorAE modifies OpenDRT and adds to it; the list of changes, with dates,
is CHANGES-FROM-OPENDRT.md in the minColorAE repository, and the unmodified
upstream file is kept there at upstream/OpenDRT_v1.1.0.dctl. Neither
minColorAE nor QCView is OpenDRT, is affiliated with the OpenDRT project or
its author, or is endorsed by them. "OpenDRT" is used only to credit the
origin of the rendering algorithm.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Derived work: darktable's AgX module (minColor AgX)
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

mincolor_agx.h and mincolor_agx.cpp port parts of darktable's AgX module
(src/iop/agx.c, src/common/custom_primaries.c)
  Copyright (C) 2025-2026 darktable developers (AgX module by István Kovács)
  https://github.com/darktable-org/darktable
  License: GPL-3.0-or-later
Ported: the lower guard rail, the log2 encoding, the sigmoid, the hue restore
and the rotated / scaled primaries geometry. The port is modified (see
CHANGES-AGX.md in the minColorAE repository) and is not affiliated with or
endorsed by darktable.

AgX is by Troy Sobotka (https://github.com/sobotka/AgX). The Blender version,
whose primaries parameters and HDR construction minColor AgX follows, is by
Zijun Eary Zhou (Eary Chow), Mark Faderbauer and Sakari Kapanen
(https://github.com/EaryChow/AgX). No code or data files are taken from those
repositories: the primaries parameters are the values published in the
headers of Blender's AgX LUTs, and the HDR method is reimplemented from its
description. "AgX" names the image formation; minColor AgX is not endorsed by
its authors.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
QCView's own additions
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

mincolor_knee.h is QCView's Highlight Knee, contributed to minColorAE and
vendored back. Everything outside src/color/mincolor/ that drives the engine
(the chain, the panel, the Metal and Direct3D hosts, the linear P3-D65 display
hand-off) is QCView's and is GPL-3.0 with the rest of the application.
